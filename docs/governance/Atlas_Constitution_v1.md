# Mission

Atlas er et Executive Intelligence Operating System.

Atlas skal gjøre virksomheter i stand til å ta bedre beslutninger basert på dokumenterte fakta, ikke antakelser.

---

# Vision

Transform data into evidence.
Transform evidence into knowledge.
Transform knowledge into decisions.
Transform decisions into measurable outcomes.
Transform outcomes into learning.

---

# Core Principles

1. Evidence before opinion.
2. Every decision must be traceable.
3. Core remains domain-neutral.
4. Domains own business knowledge.
5. Learning updates knowledge.
6. Architecture is stable; evidence evolves.
